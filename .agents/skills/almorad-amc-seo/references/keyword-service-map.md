Use slugs as *proposals* until existing routes inspected. For each route record EN/AR search intent, target reader, primary phrase, supporting subtopics, unique H1, title (pixel/visibility checked, not hard limit), meta description, intro/benefits/process, deliverables, case-study proof, FAQ, contextual links, conversion CTA and `Service` structured data only where relevant. Validate Arabic display labels separately; don't mass-produce near-duplicate pages.

The company portfolio establishes offerings but does NOT establish client testimonials, ranking metrics or particular markets beyond business context. Never claim proof unsupported by the supplied portfolio.
