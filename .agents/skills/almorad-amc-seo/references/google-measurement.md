# Google connection and analytics plan

Stage 1: site HTML, localized metadata, canonical/hreflang, sitemap/robots, performance and real service pages.
Stage 2: with domain DNS permission, verify Search Console domain property, submit sitemap, inspect sample URLs, monitor coverage, CTR, impressions, page queries and CWV.
Stage 3: configure GA4 with consent, privacy policy and no PII. Events: `view_service`, `click_book_meeting`, `booking_started`, `booking_confirmed`, `contact_submitted`, `click_get_directions`, `click_phone`, `change_language`. Ensure duplicate suppression and accurate attribution. Define booking confirmation based on committed DB booking (not button click).
Stage 4: claim legitimate Google Business Profile if eligible, validate name/address/hours/category and link contact page, gather only authentic reviews without manipulation.

Never claim account linked, site indexed, Google rankings improved, or events flowing until observed.
