# Structured data and local SEO

Eligible entities: Organization with true name/logo/url, WebSite, Service linked to real offerings, BreadcrumbList on detail pages. LocalBusiness schema ONLY after verified office name/address/phone/hours/coordinates; select most specific accurate subtype. No invented stars, opening hours, review count, awards or FAQs with guaranteed rich-result claims.

Google Business Profile eligibility and verified address/phone/hours must be checked; keep site footer, contact, Maps, emails and profile consistent. Google Maps Embed can show location; Get Directions must point to verified place. Local SEO does not mean making doorway pages for every neighborhood.

Test structured-data syntax and actual Google eligibility against official tools; syntax-valid markup alone does not guarantee special appearance.
